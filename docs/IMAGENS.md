# Imagens demonstrativas

As quatro imagens foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. São referências visuais para a POC, sem atribuição a obras reais da LCW Pisos. Nenhuma imagem de cliente foi utilizada. Os PNGs foram exportados para WebP para reduzir os arquivos do site, sem alterações editoriais adicionais.

| Arquivo final no projeto | Uso |
|---|---|
| `assets/images/living-room.webp` | Abertura, restauração, polimento, galeria e estado “depois” do comparador |
| `assets/images/living-room-before.webp` | Simulação “antes” do comparador e serviço de raspagem |
| `assets/images/reading-room.webp` | Instalação, limpeza e galeria |
| `assets/images/deck.webp` | Decks e galeria |

## Prompts finais

### living-room.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide architectural photograph for a premium Brazilian hardwood flooring landing page, purely demonstrative. Primary request: a stunning high-end modern living room with beautifully installed rich warm walnut herringbone parquet wood flooring, floor prominently occupying the bottom 55% of frame. Brazilian modernist architecture with tall floor-to-ceiling windows on the right, sculptural cream boucle sofa on the right half, subtle travertine walls and a blackened bronze column, a little tropical greenery outside. Composition: wide landscape 16:9 editorial architecture photography, eye-level low 35mm lens, open darker wall / calm negative space on the left for website typography to be overlaid later, dramatic soft late-afternoon sunlight from the right tracing the wood grain and floor pattern, realistic polished satin wood finish, luxury architectural magazine mood, highly photographic and natural. Palette warm wood brown, charcoal graphite, cream and a hint of brass. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no people. Output one photograph.

### deck.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape architectural project photograph for a premium wood flooring portfolio, demonstrative. Photograph a stunning contemporary Brazilian house garden terrace, with a large immaculate warm brown cumaru hardwood deck prominently covering the foreground, long crisp wood boards lead towards a small private pool on the right and an off-white stucco villa with floor-to-ceiling black glass doors on the left, tropical landscaping, minimal teak loungers, calm organic architecture. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, 35mm professional architectural editorial photography, afternoon warm sunlight, realistic subtle wood grain and satin protection, sophisticated clean architectural lines. No people, text, logos or watermarks. One photograph.

### reading-room.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape architectural project photograph for luxury wood floor installation reference gallery, illustrative. A beautifully curated quiet minimalist reading room of a contemporary São Paulo apartment, featuring honey oak chevron parquet wood flooring covering foreground, pale warm textured plaster walls, a single tan leather armchair and small dark wood side table at left, a large frameless window at right with soft gauzy cream curtain, floor to ceiling library recess in back wall. Architectural Digest editorial photography, rectilinear 35mm composition, subtle soft morning side lighting, peaceful sophisticated earth-tone palette, tactile grain and precise craftsmanship. Low view angle to emphasize wood floor in the lower 60 percent. Wide landscape 3:2. No people, text, logos, watermarks. One photograph.

### living-room-before.webp

Imagem-alvo: a foto demonstrativa `living-room`, gerada no primeiro prompt.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: BEFORE counterpart for a clearly labeled illustrative before/after restoration slider on a wood-flooring website. Edit target: the provided living room architectural photograph. Change ONLY the wooden herringbone flooring from its current beautiful polished condition to worn old wood needing restoration: a faded dull grayish-brown surface, visibly scuffed heavily worn varnish with small thin pale scratch marks and uneven patches along the central open floor, no huge broken holes. Keep exact same camera, dimensions, flooring board pattern, board boundaries, architecture, sun direction, furniture, windows, wall surfaces, rug, mountain view and every other object perfectly unchanged. The edit must align exactly to original so we can layer the two images for an interactive slider. Floor retains structurally intact material. This is a realistic simulation, not a real client result. No text, watermark or logos.
